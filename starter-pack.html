🦁 Starter Pack: 5 AI workflows you can set up this weekend

Five easy, low-cost workflows picked from the Roar Workflows directory. Each takes an afternoon or less. Print this page or save it as a PDF (download PDF).

1. Support ticket triage and tagging

🎧 Supportcost: lowdifficulty: easy

The problem: Agents manually tag and route every ticket.

Tools: Zendesk, Zapier, ChatGPT

  1. Trigger on new Zendesk ticket
  2. Classify category, urgency and language with an AI step
  3. Write tags/priority back to the ticket
  4. Route urgent tickets to an on-call group

Full recipe →

2. Meeting notes to CRM follow-up

💼 Salescost: lowdifficulty: easy

The problem: Call notes and next steps never make it into the CRM.

Tools: Fireflies.ai, Zapier, HubSpot, Gmail

  1. Connect Fireflies.ai to your calendar to record calls
  2. On transcript ready, trigger a Zap
  3. Summarize next steps with an AI step and log to the HubSpot deal
  4. Draft (not send) a follow-up email in Gmail for review

Full recipe →

3. Blog post to social thread repurposing

📣 Marketingcost: freedifficulty: easy

The problem: Long-form content rarely gets repurposed.

Tools: ChatGPT, Buffer, Canva

  1. Paste the post into ChatGPT with platform-specific instructions
  2. Generate threads/posts and edit for voice
  3. Create visuals in Canva
  4. Schedule in Buffer

Full recipe →

4. Receipt and invoice capture for freelancers

💰 Personal financecost: lowdifficulty: easy

The problem: Receipts get lost before tax time.

Tools: Gmail, Zapier, ChatGPT, Google Drive, Google Sheets

  1. Filter receipt emails into a Gmail label
  2. Zap extracts vendor, date, amount with an AI step
  3. Save attachments to Google Drive
  4. Append a row to a Sheets expense log

Full recipe →

5. Cited web research briefs

🔎 Researchcost: freedifficulty: easy

The problem: Quick research lacks sources.

Tools: Perplexity, Notion

  1. Ask Perplexity a focused question
  2. Check the cited sources
  3. Save the brief to Notion
  4. Re-run monthly for updates
