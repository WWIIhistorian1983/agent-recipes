Inbound lead qualification to CRM

salescost: lowdifficulty: easy

Problem

Form leads sit unscored and unrouted.

Tools

  • Typeform
  • Zapier
  • ChatGPT
  • HubSpot
  • Slack

Setup steps

  1. Trigger a Zap on new Typeform submission
  2. Send answers to a ChatGPT step with your ICP criteria; return score + reason
  3. Create/update the HubSpot contact with score
  4. Notify the owner in Slack for high scores

Check each tool's official site for current pricing and features.
