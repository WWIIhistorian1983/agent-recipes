Transaction categorization and monthly review

💰 Personal financecost: lowdifficulty: easy

Problem

Spreadsheets of transactions never get reviewed.

Tools

  • Tiller
  • Google Sheets
  • ChatGPT

Setup steps

  1. Connect accounts with Tiller to feed Google Sheets
  2. Use Tiller AutoCat rules for categorization
  3. Monthly, paste a category summary (no account numbers) into ChatGPT
  4. Ask for anomalies and a short spending review

More you can do in Personal finance

  • Auto-categorize transactions and review the month
  • Rebalance an overspent budget with an AI coach
  • Capture emailed receipts into an expense log
  • Keep small-business books categorized
