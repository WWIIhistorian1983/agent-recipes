Meeting notes to CRM follow-up

salescost: lowdifficulty: easy

Problem

Call notes and next steps never make it into the CRM.

Tools

  • Fireflies.ai
  • Zapier
  • HubSpot
  • Gmail

Setup steps

  1. Connect Fireflies.ai to your calendar to record calls
  2. On transcript ready, trigger a Zap
  3. Summarize next steps with an AI step and log to the HubSpot deal
  4. Draft (not send) a follow-up email in Gmail for review

Check each tool's official site for current pricing and features.
