SEO content brief generator

marketingcost: freedifficulty: easy

Problem

Writers start without clear search intent.

Tools

  • Google Search Console
  • Perplexity
  • Notion

Setup steps

  1. Export queries with high impressions/low CTR from Search Console
  2. Research each topic with Perplexity, keeping sources
  3. Generate a brief (intent, outline, questions) in Notion
  4. Assign to a writer
