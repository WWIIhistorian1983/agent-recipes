Support ticket triage and tagging

🎧 Supportcost: lowdifficulty: easy

Problem

Agents manually tag and route every ticket.

Tools

  • Zendesk
  • Zapier
  • ChatGPT

Setup steps

  1. Trigger on new Zendesk ticket
  2. Classify category, urgency and language with an AI step
  3. Write tags/priority back to the ticket
  4. Route urgent tickets to an on-call group

More you can do in Support

  • Auto-tag and prioritize every new ticket
  • Answer repeat questions from your help center
  • Draft replies to support emails for review
  • Get a weekly digest of ticket trends in Slack
