Training plan from your activity data

🏃 Fitnesscost: freedifficulty: easy

Problem

Generic plans ignore your actual training history.

Tools

  • Strava
  • ChatGPT
  • Google Sheets

Setup steps

  1. Export recent Strava activities
  2. Summarize weekly volume in Sheets
  3. Ask ChatGPT for a progressive plan with constraints (time, injuries)
  4. Re-review every 2 weeks; consult a professional for medical concerns
