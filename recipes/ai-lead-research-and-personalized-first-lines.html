AI lead research and personalized first lines

💼 Salescost: paiddifficulty: medium

Problem

Reps waste hours researching prospects before writing outreach.

Tools

  • Clay
  • Apollo.io
  • OpenAI API
  • Google Sheets

Setup steps

  1. Export a target list from Apollo.io
  2. Import into Clay and enrich with company/website data
  3. Add an AI column prompting for a one-line personalized opener grounded in enrichment data
  4. Review a sample manually, then push approved rows to your sequencer or sheet
